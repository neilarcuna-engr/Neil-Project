The Best Movies According to Neil Marc M. Arcuna

My Top 3 Action Movies of All Time

Mission: Impossible

Mission: Impossible is my favorite because it gives me a feeling that no other franchise really matches. There’s something addictive about the intensity of the missions and the constant tension that keeps me guessing until the very end. I love the way the movies mix smart, twist-filled storytelling with huge, practical stunts that feel real and almost unbelievable at the same time. Watching Ethan Hunt push himself to the edge pulls me into the story and makes me feel like I’m right there with him.

Rush Hour

Rush Hour is my favorite because it perfectly mixes action and comedy in a way that always makes me feel good. I love the chemistry between Jackie Chan and Chris Tucker — the way their different personalities clash and blend into something hilarious. The action is fun and creative, and the jokes always land. No matter how many times I watch it, Rush Hour always makes me laugh.

Bad Boys

Bad Boys is my favorite because it has that loud, chaotic, ride-or-die energy that no other movie delivers. Mike and Marcus are opposites but make the perfect team. Their banter, the explosions, the wild action scenes, and the deep loyalty they show each other make the movie exciting, funny, and unforgettable.
